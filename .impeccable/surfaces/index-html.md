## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is SidePlay's own display wall: real products hang on red hooks over white pegboard, each with its price on a red swing tag, and brushing past them makes them swing. Refuses the sports-shop photo banner and every dark ground.

OWN-WORLD: White perforated pegboard (grey hole grid) and white paper as the ground; signal red (#e1141f family, logo red) for hooks, swing tags, hanging signs and buttons; black only for the logo plate and text ink; prices print white on the red tags. Heavy italic uppercase Archivo for headlines and sign lettering, Archivo body, mono only for item codes. Depth from overlap and soft contact shadows only. Every tag carries the same facts in the same place.
