# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice). Must host on any static host.

## Users

People in Lebanon shopping for sports, fitness, outdoor and play gear for themselves, their kids, or their home/garden: parents buying scooters, goals or a basketball stand; home-fitness buyers; racket players; campers and hikers. Most arrive on a phone, often from Instagram/WhatsApp links.

## Product Purpose

SidePlay is a sports and play equipment store. The site is its catalog: browse the range by category, see real prices, and order in one tap through WhatsApp. Success = a visitor finds the item, sees the price, and sends an order message.

## Positioning

One store covering the whole spread of play: rackets, fitness, basketball systems, goals and backyard games, game tables, scooters/skates/boards, and camping gear, with clear USD prices shown up front (catalog prices are TTC).

## Operating Context

- Ordering: WhatsApp / phone to **71 904 725** (Lebanon, +961). Each product gets an "Order on WhatsApp" action with a pre-filled message naming the item code. No cart, no online payment.
- Physical shop / delivery coverage: **undecided** — do not claim an address, opening hours, or delivery zones until the owner confirms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog source: eight supplier price lists (`Downloads/PDF.pdf` … `PDF7.pdf`): Wish rackets; HEAD fitness; Haiyi basketball; outdoor goals & play; game tables; Discovery Adventures outdoor/camping; Cayaco scooters, skates & boards.
- Prices are USD, TTC, as listed in those catalogs. Stock/availability is not known — confirm on WhatsApp.
- Some brand names appear on supplier lists (HEAD, Wish, Discovery Adventures). Show them as the product's brand only; SidePlay is not affiliated beyond reselling.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **SidePlay** (logo: "SP" monogram with speed lines, brushed red + brushed steel on black; "SIDE" steel, "PLAY" red).
- Logo colors are binding: red, brushed silver/steel, black.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo image (supplied in chat).
- Supplier catalogs with item codes, specs and prices.
- No testimonials, customer counts, reviews, store photos, or warranty terms — never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Price and order path visible on every product — no "contact us for details" dead ends.
2. Range first: let people find their category fast (kids & play, fitness, courts & goals, ride, outdoor).
3. Phone-first: everything works one-handed on a small screen over mobile data.
4. Claims stay true: nothing about delivery, stock, or warranties until the owner confirms.
